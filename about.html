About HelpingHands NGO

Our journey, our mission, and the people making it happen

Our Story

From humble beginnings to nationwide impact – the journey of HelpingHands NGO

HelpingHands NGO was founded in 2015 by a group of socially conscious individuals who believed that real change begins at the grassroots level. What started as a small volunteer initiative in a single Delhi neighborhood has grown into a nationwide movement for social good.

Our founders, inspired by the challenges faced by underprivileged communities, decided to take action rather than wait for change. With just five volunteers and a shared vision, they began conducting free tuition classes for children in a local slum area. The overwhelming response and the visible impact on these children's lives motivated them to expand their efforts.

Over the years, we've evolved from a small education initiative to a comprehensive development organization. Today, we operate in over 15 states across India, running programs in education, healthcare, environmental conservation, women's empowerment, and skill development. Our work has touched over 50,000 lives directly and countless more indirectly.

What sets us apart is our community-driven approach. We don't just provide services – we empower communities to become self-reliant and create sustainable change from within. Every program we run is designed with input from the communities we serve, ensuring that our interventions are relevant, culturally sensitive, and truly impactful.

Our Foundation

Our Vision

A society where every individual, regardless of their socio-economic background, has access to quality education, healthcare, and opportunities for personal and professional growth. We envision communities that are self-reliant, environmentally conscious, and socially inclusive.

Our Mission

To empower marginalized communities through sustainable development programs, active citizen participation, and collaborative partnerships. We strive to create lasting positive change by addressing root causes of social issues and building capacity within communities.

Our Values

Transparency: Open communication and accountability
Empathy: Understanding and compassion in all our work
Inclusiveness: Welcoming diversity and promoting equality
Sustainability: Creating long-term impact
Integrity: Honesty and ethical practices

Our Journey & Achievements

Milestones that mark our progress and impact

2015 - The Beginning

Founded with 5 volunteers, started first education center in Delhi serving 30 children.

2017 - First Recognition

Received "Emerging NGO Award" from Delhi Government. Expanded to 3 states with 200+ volunteers.

2018 - Healthcare Initiative

Launched first medical camp program. Conducted 50 camps serving 5,000+ patients in the first year.

2019 - Green Initiative

Started environmental programs. Planted 10,000 trees and launched plastic-free campaigns.

2020 - National Recognition

Awarded "Best NGO in Education" by National Social Welfare Board. Reached 10,000 beneficiaries.

2022 - Major Expansion

Expanded to 15 states. Volunteer network grew to 2,000+. Launched women's skill development program.

2024 - Milestone Achievement

Impacted 50,000+ lives. Received UN recognition for sustainable development initiatives.

2026 - Present Day

Operating 150+ projects across India with 2,500+ volunteers. Continuing to grow and create impact.

What Drives Us

Community-Centric Approach

We believe that sustainable change comes from within communities. Our programs are designed with active participation from the people we serve, ensuring relevance and long-term impact.

Evidence-Based Programs

Every initiative is backed by research and data. We continuously monitor and evaluate our programs to ensure they're delivering measurable results and making a real difference.

Collaborative Partnerships

We work with government agencies, corporate partners, other NGOs, and local communities to maximize our impact and create synergies that benefit everyone.

Sustainable Solutions

We focus on creating lasting change rather than temporary fixes. Our programs build capacity, empower individuals, and create systems that continue to benefit communities long after our direct involvement ends.
