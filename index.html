Who We Are

HelpingHands NGO is a non-profit organization dedicated to creating positive social change across India.

Founded in 2015, we work tirelessly to uplift underprivileged communities through comprehensive programs in education, healthcare, and sustainable development. Our mission is rooted in the belief that every individual deserves access to basic necessities and opportunities for growth.

With a team of passionate volunteers and dedicated professionals, we have impacted thousands of lives by addressing root-level social issues. From providing quality education to children in remote areas to organizing free medical camps in urban slums, our work spans across multiple states and touches diverse communities.

We believe in sustainable development that empowers communities to become self-reliant. Through skill development programs, environmental initiatives, and advocacy work, we're building a foundation for long-term positive change.

Our Core Programs

We focus on three key areas that create the most significant impact in underserved communities

Education for All

We run learning centers in rural and urban slum areas, providing free education to over 2,000 children annually. Our programs include after-school tutoring, digital literacy classes, scholarship support, and career guidance for underprivileged youth.

Key Initiatives:

  • Free tuition centers in 20+ locations
  • School supply distribution programs
  • Scholarship programs for higher education
  • Digital literacy and computer training
  • Vocational training for youth
Learn More
Community Healthcare

Our healthcare initiatives bring essential medical services to communities with limited access. We organize free health check-ups, awareness campaigns, and medical camps that have served over 10,000 individuals in the past year alone.

Key Initiatives:

  • Monthly free medical camps
  • Maternal and child health programs
  • Mental health awareness sessions
  • Nutrition and hygiene education
  • Medicine distribution for chronic patients
Learn More
Environmental Action

Through tree plantation drives, waste management programs, and environmental education, we're working towards a sustainable future. We've planted over 50,000 trees and conducted 100+ awareness workshops on climate action.

Key Initiatives:

  • Annual tree plantation campaigns
  • Plastic-free community initiatives
  • Waste segregation and recycling programs
  • Clean water and sanitation projects
  • Climate change awareness workshops
